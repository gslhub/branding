# GSLHub Brand Guidelines

These guidelines define the approved use of the **GSLHub — Generative Search Lab Hub** visual identity.

## Identity

GSLHub represents independent applied research at the intersection of Generative Search, Generative Engine Optimization (GEO), AI evaluation, evidence governance and reproducible research infrastructure.

The visual system should feel **technical, rigorous, modern and research-oriented** rather than promotional or decorative.

## Core palette

| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Deep navy | `#0B132B` | Primary text, dark brand elements, research/technical tone |
| Electric blue | `#2563FF` | Primary accent, graph nodes, links and emphasis |
| Dark-mode blue | `#5B8CFF` | Accessible accent on dark backgrounds |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Negative space and dark-mode foreground |

Neutral interface colors may be used by the product UI, but the logo itself should preserve the approved brand colors.

## Logo variants

### Primary horizontal logo
Use `assets/gslhub-logo.svg` on white and light backgrounds.

### Dark-mode horizontal logo
Use `assets/gslhub-logo-dark.svg` on dark backgrounds.

### Icon
Use `assets/gslhub-icon.svg` for square placements such as GitHub, favicons, social avatars and compact UI surfaces.

## Clear space

Keep clear space around the logo equivalent to at least the height of the capital **G** in the GSL wordmark. Avoid placing text, borders or other strong visual elements inside this area.

## Minimum size

For screen use, the horizontal logo should normally be at least **140 px** wide. The icon should normally be at least **24 px** square.

## Do

- use the supplied official assets;
- preserve the original aspect ratio;
- select the light/dark variant that provides sufficient contrast;
- use the brand to identify official GSLHub projects, publications and resources;
- keep surrounding layouts simple and research-focused.

## Do not

- redraw or distort the logo;
- recolor individual logo elements without an approved brand update;
- apply gradients, shadows, bevels or decorative effects to the logo;
- rotate, stretch or crop the mark;
- use the GSLHub identity in a way that implies endorsement, partnership or authorship that does not exist;
- combine the GSLHub mark with another brand to create an unofficial composite logo.

## Naming

Preferred full name:

**GSLHub — Generative Search Lab Hub**

Short name:

**GSLHub**

The capitalization `GSLHub` should be preserved in prose and interfaces.

## Research attribution

When the logo is used in presentations, posters, papers or technical reports associated with GSLHub research, the surrounding material should identify the relevant project, authors and source repository where practical.

## Questions

For brand or collaboration questions, contact **research@gslhub.com**.
